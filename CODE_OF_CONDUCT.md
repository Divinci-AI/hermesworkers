# Code of conduct

`hermesworkers` follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) v2.1.

## In short

- Be respectful. Disagreements are fine; personal attacks are not.
- Assume good intent. Ask before accusing.
- Keep discussions on-topic — issues are for bugs and feature requests, not vendor flame wars.
- Harassment, discrimination, or sharing private information without consent are grounds for an immediate ban.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will remove comments, commits or contributions that violate this policy, and ban repeat offenders without warning.

## Reporting

Report violations through GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/security-advisories/guidance-on-reporting-and-writing-information-about-vulnerabilities/privately-reporting-a-security-vulnerability) (same channel as security reports). Maintainers will respond within 5 business days.
